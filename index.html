Why Regular / Boxy Beats Slim-Fit on You
  • Crossley et al. (2012) Ideal Male BMI: Your BMI (24.1) already matches the female-rated ideal male BMI (24.46). Slim-fit stretch fabrics collapse at the chest and hug the lower abdomen (IMG_4168), masking your solid front V-taper (~1.57 outer shoulder-to-waist ratio).
  • Architectural Drape: Midweight woven cottons (OCBDs, linen-cotton, twill) and 220–280 GSM heavyweight tees fall vertically straight from the pecs.
  • Regular Fit ≠ Oversized Sack: In IMG_0308, a super-long drop-shoulder shirt swallowed your legs. Choose regular fit in the chest/waist with shoulder seams sitting right on the bone and shirt hem ending at mid-fly (26.5"–28").

2. Stripes — Science vs. Myth

Thompson & Mikellidou (2011, i-Perception) proved the Helmholtz illusion in 3D: finely spaced horizontal stripes make figures appear ~7.1% taller & slimmer when fabric doesn't cling.

✓ GO WITH: Dark-Base Vertical (IMG_3769)

Matte Cuban-collar shirts or crisp woven button-downs in olive/black/ecru or navy/ecru vertical stripes.

✓ GO WITH: Fine Breton Micro-Stripes (Structured Knit)

Only in heavy 220+ GSM polo/tee fabric that skims straight — never thin stretch jersey.

✕ AVOID: Pale Pinstripes (IMG_2367) & Wide Rugby Belly Bands
